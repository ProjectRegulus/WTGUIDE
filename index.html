The client usually has 10–15 minutes. Meet the person first, discover their needs with open questions, and walk the space last. If you start by inspecting the building, they experience an inspection. If you start with them, they experience a business conversation — and that conversation is what closes.

Mistakes that cost the sale
  • Starting with the building instead of the person
  • Reading questions out loud from a form
  • Filling the silence after an open question
  • Giving a price in the hallway
  • Criticizing the current provider
  • Leaving without a proposal date and a review call
  • Promising on site what operations hasn't approved
